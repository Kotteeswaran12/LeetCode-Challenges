## 🎯 Why This Repository?

When I started practicing DSA, I realized that solving problems randomly is not enough.

Many different problems can be solved using the same underlying technique.

For example:

```text
Two Sum
   ↓
Two Pointers
   ↓
Three Sum
   ↓
Four Sum
   ↓
Container With Most Water
   ↓
Trapping Rain Water
```

The problems are different, but the **problem-solving patterns are reusable**.

So I decided to structure my LeetCode journey around **DSA patterns** instead of simply organizing problems by Easy / Medium / Hard.

# 🔄 From Brute Force to Optimization

One of the most important things I am learning is that the first solution doesn't always have to be the best solution.

My approach is often:

```text
Brute Force
     ↓
Understand the bottleneck
     ↓
Find a pattern
     ↓
Optimize
     ↓
Compare complexities
```

For example:

```text
O(n²)
  ↓
Find a better pattern
  ↓
O(n)
```

This process is helping me improve my problem-solving skills rather than just learning individual algorithms.
